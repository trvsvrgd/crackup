Take back Neon Docks

  1. Follow the diamond to each relay. Press H within range to sabotage it.
  2. Defeat the lieutenant, then the kingpin. Amber means an attack is coming: press Q to counter.
  3. Press H at the final core to liberate the district and earn Grapple Overdrive.

Collect green rooftop orbs to improve agility. Combat and relic discoveries earn attribute XP. Each attribute level grants one upgrade point.
